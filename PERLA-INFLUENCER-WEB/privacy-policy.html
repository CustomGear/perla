Privacy Policy

How we handle your information when you visit this site or work with Perla Desjardins.

Last updated: March 2025.

Introduction

Perla Desjardins (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, contact us for collaborations, or engage our services as a UGC (user-generated content) creator. We create content for brands that value authenticity and reach audiences including busy moms and families — and we treat your data with the same care we bring to our work.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly to us when you:

  • Fill out a contact or collaboration form
  • Send us an email or message through social media
  • Subscribe to a newsletter or updates (if offered)
  • Engage with us in connection with a brand partnership or UGC project

This information may include your name, email address, company or brand name, social media handles, project details, and any other information you choose to share. We do not collect more than is necessary to respond to your inquiry or perform the services you request.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we or our service providers may automatically collect certain information, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring URL, and pages visited. This helps us understand how visitors use the site and improve the experience. We may use cookies or similar technologies in line with your preferences and applicable law.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and collaboration requests
  • Provide UGC creation, content licensing, and related services
  • Send administrative or project-related communications
  • Improve our website, services, and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations or protect our rights

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Sharing Your Information

We may share your information only in these circumstances: (1) with service providers who assist us in operating the website or conducting our business, under strict confidentiality; (2) when required by law or to protect our rights; (3) in connection with a merger, sale, or transfer of assets, with notice as required by law. We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights or ask questions about your data, please contact us using the details below. You may also unsubscribe from any marketing communications at any time.

Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to third-party websites (for example, Instagram or other platforms). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the most recent changes. Continued use of the site or our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. For material changes, we may provide additional notice where appropriate.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact Perla Desjardins via the contact options on our Work with me page or through our official social media channels.
